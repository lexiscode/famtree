# 🌐 FAMLINK — GUEST (NON-LOGGED-IN) UI/UX BLUEPRINT

FamTree is a revolutionary digital platform designed to help families trace, connect, and preserve their lineage across generations.

In a country as culturally rich and diverse as Nigeria, where extended families often span multiple regions, languages, and traditions, FamTree bridges the generational and geographical gaps that make it difficult for people to truly know their roots.

FamTree allows families to:
- Build digital family trees that visualize their ancestry and descendants.
- Connect with linked families through marriages, showing in-law relationships.
- Host family meetings, share memories, and preserve stories in one place.

Ensure that future generations can trace their heritage with clarity and pride.

The goal is simple but powerful:
“To reconnect Nigerian families to their history, preserve their stories, and strengthen inter-generational identity through technology.”


Let’s outline the **entire guest-facing (non-logged-in)** user interface for your app **FamLink**, focusing on modern design psychology, clean flow, and emotional appeal.

The goal:

> When someone lands on the site, they should instantly feel: “This looks like something special — I want my family to be here.”


Below is a detailed breakdown of **all the key pages**, their **menu structure**, **content sections**, **text ideas**, and **design style notes** to make it sleek and high-converting.

## 🧩 DESIGN TONE SUMMARY

| Element           | Style                                                             |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Color Palette** | gold, brown, beige, or soft blues           |
| **Typography**    | Heading: Playfair Display / Body: Inter                           |
| **Feel**          | Modern tech + warm heritage                                       |
| **Imagery**       | Real Nigerian families, traditional patterns subtly in background |
| **Animations**    | Smooth fade-ins, scroll parallax, and subtle gold accents         |

### 🎨 General Design Philosophy

**Mood:** Elegant, emotional, yet professional — “Modern Family Heritage meets Tech.”
**Style:** Light theme with warm gradients (gold, brown, beige, or soft blues), subtle line art of families or roots, rounded corners, and floating glass-like sections.
**Font Pairing:** “Poppins” for headlines, “Inter” for text.
**Tone:** Heritage + Connection + Trust.
**Micro-interactions:** smooth hover transitions, slow fades, emoji icons in key spots to humanize tone.
