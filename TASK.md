Then in that page there can be a "Find Your Group" button, or something similar. The point is that, we want current and past students to be able to join a group chat for their own set. For example, "Class of 2025 (Computer Science)" group, and so on.

So from the Unilag profile homepage, once the person clicks a button, it should take them to lists of Faculties present in that university. Then a button each of those lists of faculties, to take them to a departments list page, that is all departments present in that faculty.

Then once they enter into a department, they should see lists of different student sets, like i mentioned earlier, something like say "Class of 2025 (Mechanical Engineering)", "Class of 2025 (Chemical Engineering)", etc. From 2025 down to 2020 sets.

Then the user will click on a Join button to Join one of the sets. The user will automatically be added to the group chat of that set, which would then reflect in http://localhost/messages.
